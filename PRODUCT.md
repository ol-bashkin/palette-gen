# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Designers building color palettes for design systems. They work at the intersection of design and engineering — they understand color theory, use Figma, and will paste the output directly into CSS or a token file. They are not casual users; they have opinions about chroma, lightness curves, and why OKLCH is better than HSL.

## Product Purpose

A precision tool for generating OKLCH-based color scales for design systems. Takes a base color and produces a perceptually-uniform lightness ramp, with per-shade overrides and export to CSS variables and Figma tokens. Success means a designer ships a palette they're proud of in under two minutes.

## Brand Personality

Precise, deliberate, expert. The tool does one thing and does it well. No clutter, no upsell, no onboarding wizards. The palette is the hero — the UI is its frame.

## Anti-references

- **Adobe Color**: busy, marketing-heavy, gradient-saturated chrome, features competing for attention. This tool is the opposite — the UI disappears so the palette can breathe.
- **Material Design tools**: generic, over-rounded, brand-neutral to the point of sterility. Feels like a Google demo, not a craftsperson's tool.

## Design Principles

1. **The palette is the hero.** UI chrome stays neutral and recedes. Colors are for the swatches, not the interface.
2. **Expert defaults, no hand-holding.** Sensible defaults with zero tutorial friction. Trust the user knows what OKLCH means.
3. **Precision over decoration.** Every visual element earns its place. No gradients on chrome, no decorative shadows, no flourishes that don't carry meaning.
4. **Show, don't explain.** The output renders live. No "preview" button, no modal confirmations for non-destructive actions.
5. **Restraint amplifies.** A single warm accent on a near-black surface hits harder than a full palette of UI colors.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Reduced motion already respected. Focus states must be keyboard-navigable with visible indicators. Contrast for all text against its background.
